How booking works

  1. Find a room

    Filter by building, capacity and equipment. Or enter your group size and time and let Smart Recommend rank the rooms.

  2. Send your request

    Choose a start and end date and time, enter your group size and agree to the room policy. The form checks every field before it sends.

  3. Get it confirmed

    An administrator confirms reserved bookings. You can cancel from My Bookings and say why.

What RoomOptima checks for you
